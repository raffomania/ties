# Permissions in Ties

## Lists

Lists are the central unit of organisation in ties.
They are explicitly marked as either public or private.

- Reading: Private lists are only visible to their owner and are not federated. Public lists can be read by anyone, and are visible on a user's profile.
- Editing: Only the list owner can connect/disconnect items (create/delete links), rename the list, pin or unpin it, or toggle its public/private status.


## Bookmarks

Bookmarks are considered private by default.
Once they are added to at least one public list, they are considered public.

- Reading: Private bookmarks are only visible to their owner and are not federated. Public bookmarks can be read by anyone, including their archived content.
- Editing: Only the bookmark owner can edit its title or delete it.
